# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Potential freelance clients looking for a web developer. They need to trust the developer's skills, taste, and reliability.

## Product Purpose
To showcase projects, skills, and experience with a clean, modern aesthetic, serving as the primary lead-generation tool for freelance work. Success means visitors are impressed by the craft and reach out to initiate a project.

## Positioning
A high-end, premium portfolio that demonstrates exceptional design and engineering capabilities, proving that the developer can deliver bold, creative, and top-tier web experiences.

## Operating Context
Clients will likely view this site on various devices (desktop and mobile) during the research and evaluation phase of hiring a freelancer. They might be comparing this site against other developers or agencies.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Technical stack: Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Supabase.
- Open to bold, creative design decisions; no strict constraints on the visual world at this stage.

## Brand Commitments
- Clean, modern aesthetic.
- Bold and creative experience.
